The practice02

Who is drawing

Measured, argued, then built

N Design Studio is a small interior architecture practice in Surat, Gujarat. We take on houses, apartments and small commercial interiors, and stay involved until the last shelf is level and the last snag is closed.

We measure everything, draw what already exists before proposing what doesn't, and choose materials you can hold in your hand. There is nothing inflated on this page; the work lives in the drawings and on site.

  • P·1Plan before purchase

    The cheapest decisions are the ones still made on paper. We draw until the plan is boring, then we buy.

  • P·2Materials you can touch

    No palette is chosen from a screen. Samples are approved in daylight, on site, next to the materials they will live with.

  • P·3Drawings builders can read

    Dimensions, levels and notes that survive contact with the site. A render is worthless if the workshop has to guess.

How it unfolds06

The sequence

How a project unfolds

ST·01

Listen

A long first conversation: the site, the people, the budget, the honest constraints. We would rather disappoint you at the table than on the site.

ST·02

Measure

We survey the space and draw what exists before proposing what doesn't. Levels, services, sun paths. The site always gets a vote.

ST·03

Draw

Plans, sections, material boards and a cost sheet you can argue with. Nothing proceeds to site until the drawings are boringly complete.

ST·04

Build

Supervised work, photographed and reported weekly. Site decisions happen on site, in writing, with the drawings open.

ST·05

Hand over

Snagging, styling, a measured snag list, and the complete drawings file handed over with the keys.

Five stations, in the order that keeps surprises cheap. The line fills as you read, the way the project fills as it runs.

Questions07

Answered plainly

Questions we hear first

Q·1What does N Design Studio do?
We are an interior architecture practice in Surat, Gujarat. Our work covers space planning, interior architecture and working drawings, custom furniture design, execution supervision and styling, for homes, apartments and small commercial spaces.
Q·2Which areas do you work in?
We work across Surat, including Vesu, Adajan, Dumas Road, Citylight and Pal, and take on selected projects in nearby localities.
Q·3How does a project start?
With a conversation and a site visit. We measure the space, understand how you live or work in it, and then share a written scope and fee proposal. There is no obligation at this stage. Begin here.
Q·4Do you take turnkey projects?
Yes. We execute what we draw under a single contract, with supervised work, photographed weekly progress, and written records of every site decision.
Q·5How are fees structured?
Fees depend on the scope and size of the project. We quote a fixed fee or a percentage after the first site visit, and everything is agreed in writing before work begins.
Q·6Can you work with our existing architect or contractor?
Yes. We can join your existing team and provide the interior architecture package, or assemble and supervise the full team ourselves.
